# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be friendly and welcoming to everyone, whatever their background or experience.

**Be kind.** Be respectful and patient, stay open to other views, and keep reviews about the code, not the person.

**Not okay:** harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, or
sharing someone's private information.

**Reporting:** email [stefan@stefanbogdanovic.dev](mailto:stefan@stefanbogdanovic.dev?subject=Linkwarden%20CLI%20Code%20of%20Conduct).
Reports are kept private.

**Consequences:** maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues, and warn or ban anyone who breaks these rules,
in this repository and the project's other spaces.

Based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
